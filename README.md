## 🧪 What I've Worked On

### 🌌 Introduction to Gravitational Waves and Pulsar Timing Array
*Supervised reading project, IISER Thiruvananthapuram*

A self-contained report on how millisecond pulsars act as ultra-precise cosmic clocks for detecting low-frequency gravitational waves. Completed under the guidance of **Dr. Abhimanyu Susobhanan** (School of Physics, IISER TVM).

- Covers neutron stars, pulsar emission, the interstellar medium (dispersion, scattering), pulsar timing, and PTAs
- Includes derivations such as magnetic dipole radiation, spin-down luminosity, characteristic age, braking index, and the dispersion delay equation
- Explores Hellings–Downs correlation and Fourier/χ² approaches to estimating pulse arrival times
- Written in **LaTeX**

### 🧬 Identifingers — Anvesha 2025
*Aficionados project exhibition, IISER Thiruvananthapuram's annual science festival*

A five-member team project studying the biological basis of human fingerprint patterns, combining genetics, gene expression, evolution, and statistics.

- Performed statistical analysis of fingerprint data collected from volunteers and validated pattern frequency distributions
- Designed the digital and printed project posters
- Presented the project and engaged with visitors during the exhibition
- 🏆 **First Prize, Biology Expo, Anvesha 2025**
